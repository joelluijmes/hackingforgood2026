## 2. Mapping sponsor -> approved
- 1 sponsor original (EN) -> 3 approved (EN, NL, FR) for the same audience. Sub-study folder preserved 1:1: Sponsor R1 Adult -> Approved R1 adult x3 langs; same for parents, 12-17y, 6-11y(->"8-11y", age band changed to 8-11 in approved).
- Per sub-study the 4 sponsor files -> 12 approved files. Main/R1/R2/S1 each fully covered.
- P1: 1 sponsor (parents) -> 3 approved (parents only; patients are infants).
- Pregnancy: no sponsor input; approved docs are short stand-alone information/consent for pregnant patient/partner (and their parents/guardians if minor) - ~2000 words; EN header "INFORMATION FOR PREGNANT PATIENT (PARTNER)" / "...(PARTNER)'S PARENTS/GUARDIANS", "FORUM 2.0 study". Likely derived from a CT-College pregnancy-partner template (check National Templates / Additional Materials).
- Structure: Main = general consent (all participants); R1/R2/S1/P1 = sub-study consents that REFER back to Main ("You already agreed or disagreed ... in the main ICF of the FORUM2 study"). So pipeline should treat Main + sub-study as separate documents with cross-reference.
- Sub-study content differs mostly in sections: purpose, what happens, risks, benefits, samples (R1: no extra samples; no trial-specific visits). Boilerplate (data protection, ethics, GDPR, premature end, glossary) identical across sub-studies -> good for template reuse.
- Adult vs parents approved (R1): near-identical; diff is only pronoun/person substitution ("you" -> "your child", "your researcher" -> "The researcher", "diagnosed" -> "your child has been diagnosed"), title line "FOR PARENTS/GUARDIANS", GDPR bullets "your child's data". Parents is +200 words (~3%). Mechanical transformation, automatable.
- Minors: 12-17y ~2500 words (35-37% of adult), 8-11y ~950 (14%). Radically shortened, rewritten as Q&A: 12-17 headings "What is the purpose of the SUB-study?", "WHAT WILL HAPPEN IF YOU TAKE PART?", "WHAT ARE THE POSSIBLE SIDE EFFECTS OF THE TREATMENT?", "WHO CAN YOU CONTACT IN CASE OF PROBLEMS OR QUESTIONS", "ASSENT FORM FOR..." (assent by minor + parents sign consent; data-coding explained as "Only the investigator will know which code..."). 8-11 numbered 1-6: "What is the FORUM2 R1 sub-study?", "What will happen if I participate", "Who knows that I am participating", "Has anyone checked that this study is okay?", "Do I have to participate", "To whom can you ask questions?" + simple agree line; uses "x-rays" for radiation, "Group 1 'less radiation than usual'", "A computer will decide which group". Contains image placeholders ([] / alt text, some Dutch alt text leaked).
- Sponsor minor forms are Italian-origin (Italian image captions "Immagine che contiene clipart", "Mr./Ms."): approved minors adopt same pictures + friendlier tone.

## 3. Case study: EN R1 adult  (sponsor vs approved vs national template)
Files: Sponsor `Sponsor Originals/R1/FORUM2_R1_Adult_Consent_Form_CCN_20_05_2022_EN_International V01_26.05.25.txt` (5745 w raw); Approved `Approved ICFs/R1/L1_SIS and ICF_ENG_R1_adult_v1.0_20260402.txt` (6700 w); National `National Templates/EN_Model ICF_Interv trial_Adult_Patients_version2_2_20250718.txt` (older v2: ...20250130; FR v2_3 20250925; NL v3_2 20250718).
After stripping table borders: sponsor 255 lines/5.7k words, approved 272/6.6k, template 668/14.7k.
Sentence-level reuse (sentences >=6 words, normalised exact match): of 354 approved sentences only 35 (10%) appear verbatim in sponsor; 152 (43%) verbatim from National Template; 167 (47%) in neither (= mostly trial-specific text adapted/edited from sponsor, or lightly edited template text). Sponsor sentences verbatim in template: 1. => Approved ICF is essentially the NATIONAL TEMPLATE skeleton + boilerplate, filled with sponsor SCIENTIFIC content (rewritten/paraphrased). Sponsor text is rarely copied verbatim. A pipeline must paraphrase-merge, not paste.

## 4. Relation to National Template (EN v2.2)
Template (668 lines; guidance, colour codes: purple=mandatory (adapt only with justification), black=proposal, blue=replace with trial-specific, red=guidance to delete; editorial: sentences <12 words, paragraphs <7 lines, no jargon, fixed terms "clinical trial/trial", "trial staff", "investigator", "treating physicians"). Structure: Version history -> Guidance -> Cover/"Who can I contact in case of questions?" -> "The trial at a glance" (16 guided questions: why asked/purpose; purpose of document; benefit; IMP features; painful exams; duration; side effects; insurance; pregnancy; costs; personal data; free to take part; approvals; IMP after trial; commitments; who to contact) -> CHAPTER I (15 numbered sections, ~35 subsections: 1 Why study, 2 Why asked, 3 Do I have to, 4 What will happen, 5 Benefit, 6 Risks 6.1-6.5, 7 something goes wrong, 8 new info, 9 premature end 9.1-9.4, 10 treatment after, 11 costs 11.1-11.2, 12 data 12.1-12.9, 13 biological samples 13.1-13.4, 14 meaningful health info, 15 approval) -> CHAPTER II Informed consent: PARTICIPANT prerequisites + optional numbered consents 1-11 (compensation vendor, secondary data use, genetic analyses, sample retention, additional samples, outside-trial research, cell lines, de-identification, incidental findings) -> signature blocks.
Approved R1 adult follows this skeleton exactly (same headings, same order, same chapters), with template items deleted when not applicable: IMP-specific wording (6.1 IMP side effects -> TBI, 9.2 "discontinue treatment with study drug" kept in template but approved merges/omits, 11.2 other expenses, 12.6 autologous ATMP, 13.1-13.4 reduced to one short sample section, 14 meaningful info, optional Chapter II consents 1-11). Template is for interventional trials WITH IMP in ADULT patients; FORUM2 is not a drug trial for R1 (radiation dose) so "IMP" language is adapted.
Template has version history: v2.0 (30/01/2025), 2.1 (16/05/2025) s9 fix, 2.2 (17/07/2025) s10 post-trial IMP access + 12.1 removal of word "race" (approved R1 adult says "Ethnic origin data maybe collected" - consistent with removing "race"). Approved ICFs dated 02/04/2026 so post-date template 2.2.
FR/NL template versions are separate files (FR v2.3 20250925; NL v3.2 20250718); approved FR/NL ICFs mirror their respective template wording -> for FR/NL generation, use the matching language template rather than translating the EN output.

## 5. Pipeline observations
1. Target pipeline: (sponsor EN adult) + (national template EN) -> approved EN adult; then NL/FR via national templates in those languages; then parents (mechanical pronoun swap, ~+3%), then 12-17 (~37% of adult length, Q&A, assent) and 8-11 (~14%, 6 simple questions, pictures, x-rays instead of radiation).
2. Sponsor minors/parent forms are available as inputs for each sub-study so the approved minors can be checked against them; the approved minor docs are v1.1 (later revision than adult) -> a plausible "gold" for language-level adaptation.
3. Fixed Belgian boilerplate (contact table, glossary, GDPR, insurance, approval) should be taken from template, sponsor text only supplies: title, purpose, design, risks, benefits, samples, follow-up, number of participants, contraception periods.
4. Placeholders stay unresolved in approved: "official name of site", "Surname, First name", "Phone N°" -> site-specific fields left for hospital.
5. Tables flattened in text: contact table rows split across lines; figure placeholders appear as "[]" or alt text.
6. Typos to be aware of: "Pregenancy" in filenames; "R1is", "sub- study", "maybe" in approved text (human-edited, not perfect).
