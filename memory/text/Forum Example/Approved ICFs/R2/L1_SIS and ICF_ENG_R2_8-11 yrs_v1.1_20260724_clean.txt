FORUM2 – Sub- study FORUX - Randomisation 2 (R2)

A Comparison of Ruxolitinib plus Corticosteroids versus Corticosteroids alone in children with ALL and treatment-naïve grade II-IV Graft versus Host Disease following allogeneic Hematopoietic stem cell transplantation

EU number: 2025-522052-13-00

Official title of the trial: FORUM2 – Framework for Optimizing, Refining, and Unifying Management of HSCT in Pediatric ALL

Hello,

You are invited to take part in another part of the FORUM 2 clinical study – R2 Forux sub-study. . This part of the study is done to find out what the effect of a treatment will be on your disease.

You and your parents or guardian can decide together if you want to participate in this study. You can decide to stop taking part in the research at any time. If you do not understand something or want to know more about the study, you can always ask your doctor or other persons of your choice.

1.  What is the FORUM2 R2 FORUX SUB-study?

You have a disease in your blood called leukemia. To help you get better, the doctors want to give you healthy cells from another person; It is possible t that cells from the person you receive cells from mistakenly attack your body. This has also happened to you.

This disease is treated with a medicine. In this study we want to learn if adding a second medicine can help to treat the illness better than one medicine alone. This second medicine has already been tested in other children and we want to learn more about how well it works when we start it earlier in the treatment, right when the disease begins.

2.  What will happen if I participate in the FORUM2 R2 FORUX SUB-study?

First, doctors will do some tests to make sure it’s safe for you to join. This includes things like blood tests or a other examinations. These tests and examinations will be done in your regular care, also if you don’t participate to this part of the study.

[Medicina con riempimento a tinta unita]Then you will be given medicine. You will be assigned to one of the 2 groups:

- Group 1 will take 2 medicines

  OR

- Group 2 will take 1 medicine

A computer will decide which group you will be in. Neither you, nor your parents, nor your doctor can choose the treatment. At the end of this study, the results of the two groups will be compared.

You will be monitored regularly by your doctor with examinations and blood tests. These tests will also be done if you don’t participate in this sub-study. You will regularly visit your doctor. Information about your treatment, your exams and how you feel will be collected for the sub-study. These are visits and examinations you will usually have, also if you don’t participate in the sub-study.

[]

We also find it important to understand how you feel and want to know if you are sick or have pain, during and after the treatment. Therefore, we would like to ask you to answer some questions during and after your therapy. Your parents can help you to complete these questions.

[]

Also specifically for the sub-study some blood samples will be collected during your treatment to see how your body reacts to the medicines. We will combine the blood sample with a blood sample that will already be taken as much as possible. Your information we collect for the study will receive a code without mentioning your name. The data we collect about you may also be used for later research on your disease. No extra procedures or hospital visits are needed for part of the study.

[]

If you participate to this part of the study, you will have treatment for more or less 6 months. Afterwards there is a follow up period until 2 years after you started the medication. The doctors will always check how you’re doing, just like they do now. If you ever feel something is wrong, like a headache, tummy ache, or feel sick, tell your parents right away. The doctors will take care of you and help you feel better.

3.  Who knows that I am participating in this study?

The only people who know you are participating are the doctors, the researchers and nurses looking after you, and your family doctor. All information about you is shared only with people from the study.

4.  Has anyone checked that this SUB-study is okay?

Before a study is allowed to happen, it has to be checked by a group of people who look very carefully at the study and decide if everything is okay. We call this an Ethics Committee. Only when this Committee gives an approval the study can start.

[]

5.  Do I have to participate in the R2 SUB-study?

It is important to know that you are not obliged to participate. You and your parents can decide this.

If you do not want to participate, this will have no effect on how the doctors will follow you up and you will receive your treatment as usual. Even if you have already given your permission, you can decide at any time not to participate anymore without having to say why. We do however ask you to inform us as soon as possible.

If you want to participate, please tell your parents and your doctor. In that case, we ask you to enter your name, first name and date below.
