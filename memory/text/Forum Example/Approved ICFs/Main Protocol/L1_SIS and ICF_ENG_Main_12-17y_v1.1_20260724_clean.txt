Information form for PARTICIPANTS AGED 12-17 years

FORUM2 – Master study

Framework for Optimizing, Refining, and Unifying Management of HSCT in Pediatric ALL

EU number: 2025-522052-13-00

Dear,

You are invited to take part in the main study of the FORUM 2 study, which aims to optimize the way stem cell transplantation is performed for children and young people with acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL).

This information sheet and consent form refers only to the main study of the FORUM study and the associated data collection. You are not consenting to any treatment change, experimental intervention, or clinical sub-study at this point.

Before you decide if you wish to participate, we would like you to understand why the research is being done and what it would involve for you. This document will inform you clearly and in detail about the nature and purposes of the trial, and about what taking part will mean for you, including your rights and responsibilities. A member of the team at your local hospital involved in this study will go through this information sheet with you and answer any questions that you may have. Please ask if there is anything that is not clear.

If you agree to take part, you will be asked to sign an assent form. Your parents will be asked to sign a consent form as well. You are free to withdraw at any time, without giving a reason. This will not affect the standard of care you will receive.

You will also be given a copy of this information.

What is the purpose of the study?

You have been diagnosed with ALL. Although treatment so far has led to complete remission (meaning that no leukaemia cells are currently detectable), there remains a significant risk that the leukaemia could come back based on the characteristics of your disease. To give you the best chance of a long-term cure, an allogeneic stem cell transplant (HSCT) is recommended, in which you will receive healthy stem cells from a suitable donor.

The FORUM 2 platform trial is set up by the Ospedale Pediatrico Bambino Gesù (IRCCS) in Rome (Italy). In this trial, multiple scientific questions are addressed. The platform has a main study and several study groups (sub-studies) that address different clinical questions.

This information and consent form is only about the main study of the FORUM 2 platform trial, which focuses on the collection of medical data related to your regular care HSCT for ALL. By agreeing to take part, you consent to collection of your clinical data during the regular care treatment that you will receive.

If you are eligible to participate to one of the other sub-studies, your doctor will discuss this with you and you will receive another information and assent form before your participation.

WHAT WILL HAPPEN IF YOU TAKE PART?

If you choose to participate in this main study of the FORUM2 platform trial, you will receive your stem cell transplantation and follow-up as usual. You will not have any additional tests, medical procedures, or treatments for the study.

In this main study, we ask if we can collect clinical information from your treatment such as routine examinations and treatments before, during, and after your stem cell transplantation. This includes for example, information on laboratory results, transplantation characteristics, how you are doing during the transplantation and follow-up data. These data will be stored in a secure database without mentioning your name (your name will be replaced by a code).

All visits and examinations are performed within the standard of care treatment (no study specific visits or examinations). The following is study specific:

-completion of questionnaires to evaluate quality of life (mandatory)

-blood samples (optional)

QOL questionnaires

During this study, how you are feeling will be assessed through questionnaires that need to be completed at the following timepoints:

• Before your stem cell transplantation

• After your stem cell transplantation at:

o 1 month

o 3 months

o 6 months

o 12 months

o 18 months

o 24 months

• In case you develop GvHD (graft versus host disease, a disease in which the donor cells attack the recipient's healthy tissues after a stem cell transplantation)

o At onset GvHD

o 9 months after stem cell transplantation only if you develop chronic GvHD

Specifically for the study, the FACT-BNT and Eq-5D questionnaire will need to be completed by patients from 18 years onwards. For patients between 2 and 18 years, a PEDsQL and PEDsQL Stem cell transplant module questionnaire needs to be completed. Per timepoint, this will take approximately 20 minutes to complete. For this main study, there will in total be an extra time investment of maximum 3 hours for the completion of these questionnaires (maximum 9 timepoints).

Blood samples (optional)

In addition to routine laboratory tests, study specific blood samples will be collected for Anti Thymocyte Globulin (ATG) level analysis. ATG is a medication that is given for a hematopoïtic stem cell transplantation (HSCT) to prevent that donor cells attack your healthy tissues (called graft-vs.-host-disease (GvHD)). For the study, the concentration of the active ATG will be measured in the blood at 8 different timepoints during your treatment.

Blood samples will be combined with blood samples that are taken within the routine care (no additional blood punctures or study visits needed). It is possible that an additional puncture is needed for the ATG sample that is taken after the ATG administration.

All samples will be coded to protect your identity. Your name will not be mentioned,only a special code known to your doctor. Samples will be sent to Ospedale Pediatrico Bambino Gesù, the institute that organizes the study.

Genetic tests will be performed on your samples for this study to find out what the genes in your cells look like (these genes form the 'code' in the cells that makes the cells know what to do and how to do it. This code is called a cell's 'DNA' or 'genetic material' and is uniquely constructed in each person). Possible genetic changes that can be found are very important to help us determine your treatment and thus provide essential information for the study. At the end of this form, you agree or disagree to the genetic analysis of your samples by ticking the appropriate checkbox.

It may happen that the results of the analysis of your biological samples by chance (and over and above the objectives of the study), reveal information that may be important to your health or that of your blood relatives. These data are called "accidental findings". You can choose whether you wish to be informed about this or not, and you can give your consent to this by ticking the box on the consent form on the last pages of this information letter.

What happens to leftover blood samples

We want to ask you if we can store leftover blood samples up to 25 years for future research within the context of the study (on your disease acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) and aGvHD or medication).

You can withdraw from the study at any time. This will not affect your regular care in any way. If you withdraw, you can choose for the leftover blood samples to:

- Allow your samples to be used for future research

- Or request that they be destroyed or fully anonymized (so that no one can link them back to you)

There is no direct medical benefit for you when you participate in this main study of the platform trial. However, your data obtained during your standard of care treatment may contribute to improving care and outcomes for future patients with ALL undergoing stem cell transplantation.

HOW many participants will be included in this study?

This trial will take place in approximately 26 countries and will include more than 1000 participants worldwide, including about 4-5 patients per year in Belgium.

WHAT IS THE DURATION OF THIS STUDY?

Overall, your participation in this main study will last until 5 years following your stem cell transplant during which information will be collected from your standard of care treatment.

What is expected from you?

For the study to run smoothly, it is important that you fully cooperate with the doctor and that you follow his/her instructions carefully.

If you decide to take part in this trial, you will be asked to:

- Keep all scheduled appointments

- Tell the trial doctor about any medications you take, even if it is medicine you buy without a prescription or is a natural or herbal remedy

- Tell the trial doctor if you plan to have a vaccination. Some vaccines may not be suitable during chemotherapy.

- Tell the doctor about any side effect, injury, symptom, or complaint you experience, including any unplanned hospital admissions

- Not to participate in another clinical trial without informing the investigator or trial staff

- Make sure that, if you are sexually active, you use a method of highly effective birth control both during treatment and for one year afterwards.

WHAT ARE THE POSSIBLE SIDE EFFECTS OF THE TREATMENT?

In this main study of the platform trial, you will receive the standard of care treatment. Clinical data from your standard of care treatment will be collected. Therefore, participation does not carry any direct medical risk compared to what is part of your routine care.

All medicinal products, even during standard of care treatment, can have side effects. It is very important that you report any new or worsened health problems immediately to the doctor. If you need to use other medication, discuss this with the investigator beforehand. If, for any reason, you consult another treating physician during the trial you must inform him/her that you are taking part in a trial. This could be important in determining a diagnosis and giving you the correct treatment if needed.

WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS and what happens if you do not participate in this study?

Your decision to participate in this study is entirely voluntary. If for any reason and at any time you decide not to participate, you can tell your doctor. Your doctor and the nurses won't mind if you don't want to participate. You will then be further treated according to the same standard of care treatment that you are already receiving but no data will be collected anymore. Any information we have already collected on you may still be used.

The doctor conducting this study will inform your general practitioner and/or pediatrician about your participation in this study.

This study was evaluated by an Ethics Committee in Belgium. The study is thus carried out in accordance with national laws. Your data will be treated confidentially and given a code (pseudonymized) in order to respect your privacy.

This means that other people cannot directly see that it belongs to you. Only the investigator will know which code belongs to which person. Other people will only see the code, so they won’t know your name. This coded data can then be shared with other researchers for future studies in the context of the same or similar disease or treatment. These studies may take place in Belgium or abroad. Such new studies must always first be approved by the ethics committee. If you do not want your data to be used for future research, you can let your parents or the doctors know.

The sponsor will also ensure that insurance (protection against unexpected costs) is taken out to protect and/or treat you in the event of damage and/or injury resulting from your participation in this study.

You can ask questions about the study at any time.
