Information form for Children 8-11 years

FORUM2 – Main study

Framework for Optimizing, Refining, and Unifying Management of HSCT in Pediatric ALL

EU number: 2025-522052-13-00

Hello,

You are invited to take part in the FORUM 2 clinical study, a study that is done to further improve the treatment for your disease. This information is only about the participation in the main part of the FORUM 2 study.

You and your parents or guardian can decide together if you want to participate in this study. You can decide to stop taking part in the research at any time. If you do not understand something or want to know more about the study, you can always ask your doctor or other persons of your choice.

1.  What is the FORUM 2 study?

You have a disease in your blood called leukemia. To help you get better, the doctors want to give you healthy cells from another person, a so called stem cell transplantation.

The FORUM 2 study has a main study and different other parts that all have the aim to further improve the treatment of patients with a leukemia that need a stem cell transplantation.

This information is only about the main study of the FORUM 2 trial. If you take part, in the main study, you will receive your treatment as usual. Some information will be collected from your treatment as usual to learn from this.

If your doctor thinks you can participate to other parts of this study, you will receive another information sheet to explain this to you.

This trial will take place in approximately 26 countries and more than 1000 participants worldwide will take part. About 4-5 patients per year in Belgium will take part.

2.  What will happen if I participate in the MAIN PART of THE FORUM 2 study?

If you choose to participate in this main study of the FORUM 2 study, you will receive your stem cell transplantation and follow up as usual. You will not have any additional tests, medical procedures, or treatments for the study.

[]

The doctors will always check how you’re doing, just like they do now. If you ever feel something is wrong, like a headache, tummy ache, or feel sick, tell your parents right away. The doctors will take care of you and help you feel better.

In this main study, we ask if we can collect information from your treatment before, during, and after your stem cell transplantation. This includes for example, information on results of tests on your blood and how you are feeling. All this information will be collected from your medical notes of the hospital and the information will be stored safely. This means your information will receive a code without mentioning your name.

[]

We also find it important to understand how you feel and want to know if you are sick or have pain, during and after the treatment. Therefore, we would like to ask you to answer you some questions during and after your therapy. Your parents can help you to complete these questions.

[]

Also specifically for the study some blood samples will be collected during your treatment to see how your body reacts to the treatment. We will combine the blood sample with a blood sample that will already be taken as much as possible. Your information we collect for the study will receive a code without mentioning your name. The data we collect about you may also be used for later research on your disease. No extra procedures or hospital visits are needed for this study.

[]

Overall, your participation in this main study of the trial will last until 5 years after your stem cell transplantation. Information will be collected from your treatment.

3.  Who knows that I am participating in this study?

The only people who know you are participating are the doctors, the researchers and nurses looking after you, and your family doctor. All information about you is shared only with people from the study.

4.  Has anyone checked that this study is okay?

Before a study is allowed to happen, it has to be checked by a group of people who look very carefully at the study and decide if everything is okay. We call this an Ethics Committee. Only when this Committee gives an approval the study can start.

[]

5.  Do I have to participate in the study?

It is important to know that you are not obliged to participate. You and your parents can decide this.

If you do not want to participate, this will have no effect on how the doctors will follow you up and you will receive your treatment as usual. Even if you have already given your permission, you can decide at any time not to participate anymore without having to say why. We do however ask you to inform us as soon as possible.

If you want to participate, please tell your parents and your doctor. In that case, we ask you to enter your name, first name and date below.
