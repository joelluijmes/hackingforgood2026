INFORMATION FOR PREGNANT PATIENT (PARTNER)’S PARENTS/GUARDIANS

FORUM 2.0 study

Official title: FORUM2 – Framework for Optimizing, Refining, and Unifying Management of HSCT in Pediatric ALL

EU CT number: 2025-522052-13-00

Sponsor of the study: Ospedale Pediatrico Bambino Gesù, IRCCS, Piazza di Sant’Onofrio 4, Rome, Italy

Dear,

Your child/your child’s partner is taking part in the FORUM 2.0 study, and have informed us that your child/your child’s partner is pregnant.

Your child’s doctor explained you that, due to the treatment of your child’s (partner) there might be some risks for the baby. Therefore we would like to collect some information about the pregnancy in the scope of the FORUM 2.0 study.

Before you decide whether you would like to consent to this information collection, it is important to understand why it is being done and what it will involve. A member of the team at your child’s (partner) local hospital involved in this study will go through this information document with you and answer any question that you may have. Please ask if there is anything that is not clear.

If you agree to data collection, you will be asked to sign a consent form. You are free to withdraw your consent at any time, without giving a reason.

You will receive a copy of this information document.

1.  What is the purpose of collecting information regarding your child’s (partner) pregnancy and its outcome?

We request your consent to collect information because your child became pregnant while she / her partner participated in a clinical trial.

This treatment might have effect on the baby during the pregnancy. Therefore, not all of risks and side effects are known. Currently, it is not known whether this treatment has an effect on human sperm or egg cells and/or on an unborn baby. Thus, for security reasons, we wish to collect information about your child and her pregnancy.

2.  What does your agreement for sharing data mean to you?

We will collect the following information:

- Start of the last menstruation

- Expected date of birth

- Contraception use

- Outcome of the birth

Your child’s medical information, as well as that of your child’s baby, will be collected up to 30 days after your child’s baby's birth, or longer if deemed necessary by the sponsor.

The physician-researcher will only keep records of your child or your child’s baby that are collected anyway as part of the standard pregnancy follow-up with the gynaecologist, midwife and family physician. No additional examinations or tests will be performed on your child by the study staff.

We also hereby request your permission for the physician-investigator to contact the physicians in charge of the care of the pregnancy and the unborn baby. In addition, we will also contact your child at the time the pregnancy is expected to end so that we may receive further information regarding the outcome of the (partner) pregnancy, birth and the health of the baby.

We would also ask you to contact the physician-investigator if your child decides to terminate the pregnancy for any reason, or if the pregnancy is terminated unexpectedly.

3.  What are the risks and expected benefits?

There are no medical risks to your child in sharing information about the pregnancy and its outcome. In this observational part of the study, the only potential risk would be a breach in the measures taken in order to protect the confidentiality of your child’s private data. That risk is controlled by the measures detailed under point 5. Confidentiality.

Sharing information about the pregnancy and its outcome is unlikely to bring any immediate therapeutic benefit. However, your child may help to better help other patients in the future and to identify the effects of exposure to the drug(s) in pregnant women and their unborn babies.

4.  Are there any costs if your child participates in this pregnancy information collection?

There are no additional costs if your child participates in this pregnancy information collection. You will not receive any financial compensation or reimbursement of travel expenses for your child taking part in this clinical study.

5.  Confidentiality

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (or GDPR – EU 2016/679) of 27^(th) of April 2016 and the Belgian law of 30^(th) of July 2018 regarding the protection of individuals related to the processing of personal data, your child privacy will be respected and you can have access to the data collected if you want. Each error can be corrected at your request.

All information collected during this study will be pseudonymised (pseudo-anonymized meaning we can link your child’s data back to your child’s personal file). The database containing the results of the study will therefore not contain any combination of elements such as your child’s initials, gender and your child’s full date of birth (dd/mm/yyyy). The key to the code assigned to your child will only be accessible to the investigator/treating physician, or the nurse/study coordinator. Only the pseudonymised data will be used in any type of documentations, reports or publications (in the medical scientific literature and/or at medical conferences) concerning this study. The confidentiality of the data will thus always be guaranteed.

Your child’s (partner) trial number will be passed on to personnel at the laboratories doing the tests to help them identify the blood samples.

Personal data and data regarding your child’s pregnancy will be processed and stored during at least 25 years. The responsible data processor and the manager of the database is the promotor of the study, the Ospedale Pediatrico Bambino Gesù, in Rome (Italy). The study is organised by the Ospedale Pediatrico Bambino Gesù, IRCCS, Piazza di Sant’Onofrio 4, Rome (Italy). If you withdraw your consent for your child to take part in the pregnancy information collection, to guarantee the validity of the research, the data encoded up to the point at which you withdraw will be retained. No new data may be sent to the sponsor.

Representatives of the promoter, auditors, the Medical Ethics Commission and the Competent Authorities, all bound by professional secrecy, can have direct access to your child medical records under the responsibility of the investigator (or one of his/her collaborators) in order to check the study procedures and/or the data, without violating its confidentiality. This is only possible within the limits of the relevant laws and under the responsibility of the research-doctor and his/her supervision (or one of his/her study coordinators). By signing this consent form and having received the preliminary explanations, you also consent to this access.

Pseudonymised data (data linked only using your child unique trial number) may be provided to other 3rd parties (e.g. pharmaceutical companies or other academic institutions) for research and safety monitoring. They have the same duty of confidentiality to you and your child as other personnel. These regulatory authorities or research organisations could be within this country or abroad. This may be to countries outside of Europe where data protection regulations differ. Any such request is carefully considered by the study researchers and will only be granted if the necessary procedures and approvals are in place. This information will not identify your child and will not be combined with other information in a way that could identify your child. The information will only be used for the purpose of health research, and cannot be used to contact you or your child or to affect it’s care. It will not be used to make decisions about future services available to you, such as insurance.
