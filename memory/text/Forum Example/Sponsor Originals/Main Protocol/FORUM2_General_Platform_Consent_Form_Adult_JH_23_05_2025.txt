B.  INFORMATION SECTION

SUMMARY OF THE TRIAL: KEY INFORMATION

You are invited to participate in the FORUM 2 platform study, which focuses on the standardized documentation of medical data related to allogeneic stem cell transplantation (HSCT) for acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL). By agreeing to take part, you consent to the structured and continuous collection of your clinical data. This documentation is essential to help researchers understand treatment outcomes and improve care for future patients.

FORUM 2 is a platform study. This means it provides a shared infrastructure to address multiple scientific questions over time, within a single framework. Some patients may be invited to participate in specific sub-studies already included in the platform, such as comparisons of different conditioning regimens (e.g., Group R1), prevention or treatment strategies for graft-versus-host disease (e.g., Group R2), or observational assessments (e.g., Groups O1 and O2). In addition, investigator-initiated "add-on" studies may be introduced later. If you become eligible for any of these additional studies, you will be contacted separately.

This information sheet and consent form refer only to the general participation in the FORUM 2 platform and the associated data collection. You are not consenting to any treatment change, experimental intervention, or clinical sub-study at this point. Every further study or intervention will be explained in detail, and your explicit written consent will be requested again before participation.

Your participation in the platform is entirely voluntary. You can withdraw your consent at any time without providing a reason, and this will not affect the quality or continuity of your medical care.

C.  FURTHER INFORMATION

1.  What is the purpose of the trial?

The FORUM 2 study is a platform trial designed to improve the care of patients with acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) who undergo allogeneic stem cell transplantation (HSCT). Its primary aim is to support standardized, high-quality documentation of transplant procedures, treatment courses, and clinical outcomes across all participating centers. This platform is composed of several defined study groups ("sub-studies") that each focus on specific clinical questions. These include, for example, comparisons of different conditioning regimens, approaches to preventing or managing graft-versus-host disease (GvHD), evaluation of risk factors, and post-transplant interventions. While not every patient will participate in each of these sub-studies, all participants in the platform contribute to the shared dataset through uniform documentation and follow-up.

A platform approach is especially suitable for rare and pediatric diseases like ALL, where data acquisition is complex and time-consuming. To ensure sufficient data quality and scientific value, FORUM 2 operates as a multinational, multicenter initiative involving leading transplant centers around the world. In addition to the planned sub-studies, the platform may occasionally include smaller, investigator-initiated “add-on” studies in the future. Participation in any such additional project would always require separate information and specific consent.

This consent refers solely to your participation in the platform framework and the associated routine documentation. It does not cover any specific intervention or clinical research question.

2.  What are the patient groups compared? What is the intervention being tested?

This consent form concerns only your inclusion in the general data collection framework of the platform study.

The platform itself consists of several predefined study groups that address different clinical questions. These include, for example:

- Group R1: comparing two different radiation doses used in conditioning therapy prior to HSCT

- Group R2: evaluating treatments for acute graft-versus-host disease (aGvHD)

- Group S1: assessing different immune suppression strategies for transplants from mismatched family donors

- Group O1 and O2: observational cohorts studying transplant-related risk factors

- Group P1: a pilot study on post-transplant maintenance therapy in young children

Each of these study groups has its own specific eligibility criteria, timeline, and consent form. You will only be asked to participate in one of them if you meet the relevant criteria, and only after receiving a separate, detailed explanation and giving your explicit written consent.

3.  What examinations, tests and procedures are provided for if we authorise participation in the trial?

Participation in the FORUM 2 platform does not involve any additional tests, medical procedures, or treatments beyond those that are part of your standard clinical care. No experimental medication or intervention is being given in connection with this consent.

The only difference is that selected clinical information from your routine examinations and treatments before, during, and after your stem cell transplant will be documented in a standardized format. This includes, for example, information on laboratory results, transplant characteristics, complications, and follow-up data. These data will be collected from your medical records and stored in a secure, pseudonymized database.

If you are invited to participate in a specific sub-study or clinical intervention in the future, additional procedures may be required. In that case, all procedures will be fully explained to you, and a separate consent form will be provided.

Furthermore, female patients of childbearing potential must agree to adhere to effective contraception during the study period

4.  What risks can I face if I participate in the trial?

General Access to the FORUM 2 platform is limited to the structured collection and storage of your clinical data. Therefore, participation does not carry any direct medical risk beyond what is already part of your routine care. Access is limited to authorized personnel, and the data will not be traceable to you personally.

There is no direct medical benefit for you from participating in the platform alone. However, your data may contribute to improving care and outcomes for future patients with ALL undergoing stem cell transplantation. Should you participate in a sub-study in the future, potential risks and benefits would be explained separately.

5.  How will I be notified of any unexpected results following diagnostic tests?

Participation in the FORUM 2 platform does not involve any additional diagnostic procedures beyond your routine medical care. Therefore, it is not expected that the data collected within the platform will lead to any unexpected or incidental medical findings.

However, if—for any reason—clinically relevant information was to emerge during the evaluation of your routinely collected medical data, the responsible physician at your treatment center will inform you and discuss any consequences, if appropriate. You may also be asked whether you wish to be informed about such findings, particularly if they are not directly related to your current treatment.

Any future sub-study involving specific testing (e.g., genetic analysis or research-specific diagnostics) will provide a detailed explanation of potential incidental findings and offer you the option to receive or decline that information.

6.  Is it useful/necessary to inform my family doctor/pediatrician?

Yes, it is important that your family doctor/pediatrician or general practitioner (GP) is informed about participation in this clinical trial. Your family doctor may play an important role in supporting your general health, identifying possible side effects, and helping manage medications during longer-term follow-up.

However, please note that during the period of conditioning, transplantation, and early recovery, the transplant center will be your primary point of contact and care. All treatment decisions, monitoring, and any urgent interventions will be coordinated and managed by the specialized transplant team. After the first phase is completed, you will continue to be followed by the transplant center in regular visits up to 5 years after transplant. Your family doctor is welcome to remain involved and may be consulted at any time, but during this time, the transplant center team remains fully responsible for your care.

7.  What will my commitment be and what are my responsibilities if I decide to participate in the trial?

- Make sure that you attend all scheduled visits up to 5 years after transplant.

- Inform the investigator of any changes in your current medications, illnesses or injuries, unexpected or disruptive side effects, problems that occur during the study and hospitalizations.

8.  Will I have to bear costs for participation in the trial? Will I be reimbursed for any expenses? Will I receive a compensation?

You will not have to pay any costs related to your participation in this clinical trial. All procedures, treatments, and assessments required by the study are fully covered by the transplant centre and/or the study sponsor.

Participation in the study does not involve any form of financial compensation. According to applicable laws and ethical guidelines, it is not permitted to offer payment or rewards for taking part in clinical research.

10. Who will have access to our child’s/the minor’s health data, including personal data, and how will they be treated during the trial?

Your data, in particular personal and health data, will be processed only to the extent that they are indispensable to the objective of the trial and for pharmacovigilance purposes, in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/679, known as GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and with Legislative Decree 10 August 2018, no. 101. In practical terms, the documents relating to the participant will be kept in a safe place and will not report his/her name in clear text, only known to the researchers, but an identification code.

The data, made anonymous, may be subject to control by regulatory bodies and used for scientific publications (journals, conferences).

Your clinical data collected for the purposes of the trial, as well as the results of the tests carried out, will be stored for the time required by the regulations and subsequently destroyed. They will not be destroyed only if a) it is no longer possible to trace them back to your identity, because they have been anonymised during the trial itself; b) in the presence of your specific informed consent.

If the personal data are transferred to a third country or to an international organization, all the guarantees will be adopted which are provided for by Article 46 of the GDPR 679/2016.

Further information is included in the attached data processing authorization form.

11. How will your biological samples taken for trial purposes be treated and who will have access to them?

Participation in the FORUM 2 platform does not require the collection of any additional biological samples beyond what is already part of your routine medical care. However, if biological samples such as blood or tissue are taken during standard procedures (e.g., during transplantation or follow-up), selected information derived from those analyses may be included in the platform database.

No additional analyses will be performed on your samples specifically for research purposes within this general platform participation. Any future study involving the use or storage of biological material for scientific evaluation will be explained to you in detail, and your separate written consent will be requested.

12. How will I have access to the results of the trial?

After the trial has ended and all data have been collected and analysed, the results will be summarized and shared with the scientific and medical community through publications and presentations. These results will help improve future treatment strategies for children and adolescents with acute lymphoblastic leukemia.

According to applicable regulations, you have the right to access the general results of the trial. If you wish, you may ask the study doctor to provide you with a summary of the study’s findings once they are available. Alternatively, the study team can inform you where and how the results will be published or made publicly accessible. Please note that your individual results will not be part of these summaries, and all information will remain pseudonymized to protect your privacy.
