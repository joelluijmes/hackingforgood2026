A.  INTRODUCTION

Dear .........................,

your parents have been involved to inform them and ask for their consent to your participation in the FORUM 2 research platform. The aim of this platform is to collect and document medical information from patients undergoing stem cell transplantation (HSCT) for acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) in a standardized way.

This consent refers only to the participation in the platform and the collection of your clinical data. You are not consenting to any specific treatment or intervention. Separate consent will be required for any additional study or procedure in the future.

Your participation is entirely voluntary. If you choose not to take part, your medical care will not be affected in any way.

The Principal Investigator

B.  INFORMATION SECTION

SUMMARY OF THE TRIAL: KEY INFORMATION

By agreeing to participate, you give permission for your doctors to collect and share details about your medical condition, treatment, and recovery. This information will help researchers better understand how to improve care for patients like you in the future.

FORUM 2 is a platform study, which means it provides a structure for several smaller studies that may happen now or later. Some patients may be invited to join these sub-studies, which explore specific medical questions. For example:

- Group R1 compares different doses of radiation before transplant.

- Group R2 looks at how to prevent or treat a complication called graft-versus-host disease.

- Groups O1 and O2 are observational and simply collect information to study risk factors or outcomes.

- Group S1 studies immune suppression strategies for certain donors, and Group P1 looks at therapy after transplant in younger children.

Right now, you are only being asked to take part in the main platform study, which involves the collection of your clinical data. You are not being asked to join any treatment trial or make any changes to your current care at this time.

If, in the future, you are eligible for one of the sub-studies, you and your parents will be given full details and asked for separate permission before participating.

Participation is voluntary. You may choose whether or not to take part. If you decide to participate now, you can still change your mind at any time. Saying no will not affect your care or your relationship with your doctors in any way.

C.  FURTHER INFORMATION

1.  Trial Authorization

  The trial protocol proposed to you has been examined and approved by the Ethics Committee ......................... Among other things, the Ethics Committee has verified the compliance of the trial with the standards of Good Clinical Practice and with the ethical principles expressed in the Declaration of Helsinki and that your safety, rights and well-being have been protected.

2.  Processing of personal, health and clinical data

  Your data, in particular personal and health data, will be processed only to the extent that they are indispensable to the objective of the trial and for pharmacovigilance purposes, in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/679, known as GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and with Legislative Decree 10 August 2018, no. 101, that is, the rules governing privacy. In practical terms, the documents concerning you will be kept in a safe place and will not report your name in clear text (only known to the researchers), but an identification code.

  The data, made anonymous, may be subject to control by regulatory bodies and used for scientific publications (journals, conferences).

  Your clinical data collected for the purposes of the trial, as well as the results of the tests carried out, will be stored for the time required by the regulations and subsequently destroyed. They will not be destroyed only if a) it is no longer possible to trace them back to your identity, because they have been anonymised during the trial itself; b) in the presence of your specific informed consent.

  If the personal data are transferred to a third country or to an international organization, all the guarantees will be adopted which are provided for by Article 46 of the GDPR 679/2016.

3.  Treatment of biological samples

  Participation in the FORUM 2 platform does not require the collection of any additional biological samples beyond what is already part of your routine medical care. However, if biological samples such as blood or tissue are taken during standard procedures (e.g., during transplantation or follow-up), selected information derived from those analyses may be included in the platform database.

  No additional analyses will be performed on your samples specifically for research purposes within this general platform participation. Any future study involving the use or storage of biological material for scientific evaluation will be explained to you in detail, and your separate written consent will be requested.

4.  Access to the results of the trial?

  Once the trial is over and all the resulting data have been collected, they will be analyzed to draw conclusions. The investigators undertake to make them available to the scientific community.

  The law provides for the possibility that participants have access to the results of the trial. Therefore, you and your parents can ask the investigating physician to communicate the general results of the trial.

5.  Unexpected results following analysis

  Being part of the FORUM 2 platform does not require you to have any extra tests beyond those already planned for your usual medical care. Because of this, it is unlikely that we will discover anything unexpected in the data collected.

  However, if your doctors notice something important for your health while reviewing your regular medical information, they will inform you and explain what it might mean. In some cases, you may be asked whether or not you would like to know about these findings, especially if they are not directly related to your current treatment.

  If, in the future, you are invited to take part in a sub-study that involves special tests—such as genetic analysis or other research-related procedures—you will be given full information, including whether any unexpected findings could occur. You will also be given the choice to decide whether or not you want to be told about those results.
