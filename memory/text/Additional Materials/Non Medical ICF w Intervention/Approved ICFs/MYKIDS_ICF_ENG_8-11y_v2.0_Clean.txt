
Information form for children (8-11 years)

Title of the study: MYKIDS

Official title: MyKids: Molecular identification and characterisation of non-rhabdomyosarcoma soft-tissue sarcomas in children, adolescents and young adults: an EpSSG NRSTS study.



Hello,


You are invited to take part in a clinical study. This is scientific research that is done to find out what effect new medications have on your disease. You and your parents or guardian can decide together if you want to participate in this study. If you do not understand something or want to know more about the study, you can always ask your doctor or other persons of your choice.

WHY AM I INVITED TO TAKE PART IN THIS STUDY?

You have been invited to take part in this study because you (possibly) have a soft tissue tumour.  These tumours can appear anywhere in the body.

WHAT IS THE GOAL OF THIS THIS STUDY?

We want to understand where the rare tumours that grow in the soft tissues of children and young people come from. By learning this, we can help doctors to better treat children with these tumours in the future.




WHICH TESTS WILL BE DONE FOR THE STUDY?



The surgeon will remove the entire tumour or a small piece of tumour tissue (biopsy) from you under general anaesthesia (medical treatment that will put you in a very deep sleep). This may already have taken place. In that case we shall request a piece of your tumour biopsy from the biobank. We will test the characteristics of your tumour. A is like a big library, but instead of keeping books, it stores small parts of the human body, like blood or pieces of skin. Scientists also write down lots of information about these samples to better understand diseases and find new medicines.

During the study they will do some tests on your blood. Via these samples the doctors want to test if they can find out if you have cancer through blood tests. The goal is in the future to detect cancer earlier trough a blood test. 
No extra pricks are therefore required for the study. 

All samples taken for the study will be given a code so they do not mention your name. This way they can be stored for 25 years and later be used for further research into your disease. You can ask to have the collected samples destroyed at any time without having to give a reason for this.

All data we collect about you will be coded (pseudonymized), so no one outside the study team will know your name. The data we collect about you may also be used for later research on your disease.

WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DO NOT WANT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE STUDY?

If you do not want to participate in the study or if your parents do not want you to participate in the study, you can say this to your doctor. Your doctor and the nurses will not mind if you don’t want to take part. The doctor will then look with you how your disease will be further treated according to the current standard. 
You can always ask questions about the study at any time.

TO WHOM CAN YOU ASK QUESTIONS?

If you have any questions about the study during your treatment or if there are any problems, you can always talk to your parents or guardian, the nurses and your doctor:
